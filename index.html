what we build

Three kinds of work, one small team.

We keep our scope narrow on purpose. No apps, no platforms we can't stand behind, just websites done well from the design through to the database.

Business websites

frontend + backend

Full front end and back end for businesses of any kind, including the design work most cheap builds skip. That covers layout, brand colors and type, the pages themselves, and the server-side logic behind contact forms, bookings, logins, or anything else your site needs to actually do.

Every site we ship is mobile compatible from the first sketch, not adjusted afterward.

Forms and data logging

an alternative to Google Forms

Quiz forms, intake forms, and personalized data logging forms, built to match your brand instead of looking like a generic form template. We also set up and manage the system the data lands in, so you get a clean, usable record rather than a spreadsheet you have to clean up yourself.

Research, turned into a website

for colleges and research groups

Have a research topic, paper, or project that deserves more than a PDF nobody opens. We build it out as a proper website: a link you can share, that reads well, and that students and the public can actually get through. Good for college research groups and independent science projects alike.
